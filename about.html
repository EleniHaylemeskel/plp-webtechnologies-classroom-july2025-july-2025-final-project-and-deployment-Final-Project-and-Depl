About Cookies Corner

Cookies Corner was created to bring something fresh to Ethiopia’s gifting culture—personal, heartfelt treats delivered with care. We make gifting simple, meaningful, and fun through our online cookie builder, themed gift boxes, and monthly cookie surprises.

What We Make

We craft premium cookies that do more than taste good. Each treat can carry a short embedded message, and customers can choose from themed boxes or sign up for monthly cookie surprises designed for different moods and occasions.

Who We Serve

Our products are made for urban professionals, parents, event planners, and online shoppers seeking thoughtful, convenient, and personalized gifting experiences. As Ethiopia’s digital adoption grows, we’re helping lead the movement for unique, meaningful food gifts.

What Makes Us Different

  • Fully customizable cookies through our online builder
  • Eco-friendly packaging
  • Social impact: training and employment opportunities
